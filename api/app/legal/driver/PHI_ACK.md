# Driver PHI Confidentiality And Privacy Acknowledgment

## Purpose

As part of my duties as a driver, I may have access to confidential information concerning passengers,
patients, members, or clients. This information may include Protected Health Information ("PHI") and
other confidential personal information.

I understand that I am responsible for protecting this information and may use or disclose it only as
permitted by company policy, applicable contracts, and applicable federal and state law.

## Protected And Confidential Information

I understand that PHI and confidential information may include:

- Passenger names, addresses, telephone numbers, dates of birth, and other identifying information
- Medicaid, Medicare, insurance, member, or identification numbers
- Medical appointments, treatment locations, medical facilities, providers, or destinations
- Information concerning a passenger's medical condition, disability, mobility needs, treatment, or health status
- Trip information that reveals or could reveal that an individual is receiving health care or medical services
- Information contained in dispatch systems, scheduling systems, mobile applications, manifests, trip sheets, electronic communications, or other company records
- Conversations or communications concerning passengers and their health care or transportation

## My Responsibilities

I understand and agree that I will:

- Access confidential information only when necessary to perform my assigned duties
- Use or disclose PHI and confidential information only for authorized business purposes and only to persons authorized to receive it
- Protect passenger information from unauthorized access, viewing, disclosure, loss, or theft
- Not discuss passengers, their medical conditions, appointments, destinations, or other confidential information with friends, family members, other passengers, unauthorized employees, or other unauthorized persons
- Avoid discussing confidential passenger information where conversations may reasonably be overheard
- Protect trip manifests, schedules, passenger lists, mobile devices, paperwork, identification information, and other materials containing confidential information
- Not photograph, screenshot, copy, download, transmit, post, or retain passenger information except when specifically authorized for legitimate business purposes
- Not post passenger information, photographs, trip details, or other confidential information on social media or other public platforms
- Use only authorized systems, applications, devices, and communication methods for accessing or transmitting confidential information
- Protect passwords, PINs, and other access credentials and not permit another person to use my credentials
- Secure or properly dispose of documents and materials containing confidential information according to company policy
- Immediately report any suspected or actual loss, theft, unauthorized access, disclosure, misdirected communication, compromised device, or other privacy or security incident
- Cooperate with investigations concerning suspected privacy or security incidents

## Minimum Necessary Information

When applicable to my duties, I will access, use, or communicate only the minimum amount of confidential
information reasonably necessary to perform the transportation service or other authorized job function.

## Passenger Privacy

I understand that transporting a passenger does not give me permission to access, inquire about, discuss,
or disclose the passenger's medical information beyond what is reasonably necessary to safely and properly
perform my assigned duties.

I will treat passenger information and conversations concerning passengers as confidential.

## Continuing Confidentiality

My responsibility to protect confidential information continues after a trip has ended and after my
employment, contract, or other relationship with the company ends.

I will not retain, access, use, or disclose passenger information after it is no longer required for my
authorized duties.

## Violations

I understand that unauthorized access, use, disclosure, copying, retention, or distribution of confidential
information may violate company policy, contractual requirements, and applicable law.

Violations may result in corrective or disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment
or contractual relationship, as well as other consequences permitted by applicable law.
