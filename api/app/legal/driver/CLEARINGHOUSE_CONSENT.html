Driver Consent

I, {fullname}, hereby provide my consent to the employer identified above, and/or its authorized Consortium/Third-Party Administrator (C/TPA), to conduct limited queries of the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) Commercial Driver's License Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse ("Clearinghouse") to determine whether drug or alcohol violation information about me exists in the Clearinghouse.

I authorize the employer to conduct limited queries for the duration of my employment with the employer, beginning on the date I sign this consent.

I understand that a limited query will indicate whether information about me exists in the Clearinghouse but will not disclose the specific details of any drug or alcohol violation information contained in my Clearinghouse record.

If a limited query indicates that information about me exists in the Clearinghouse, I understand that the employer must conduct a full query in order to obtain additional information from my Clearinghouse record.

I understand that this consent does not authorize a full query. Before the FMCSA Clearinghouse releases detailed violation information to the employer through a full query, I must provide specific electronic consent through the FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse.

I further understand that if I refuse to provide consent for the employer to conduct a required limited query of the Clearinghouse, the employer must prohibit me from performing safety-sensitive functions, including operating a commercial motor vehicle, as required by applicable FMCSA drug and alcohol testing regulations.
