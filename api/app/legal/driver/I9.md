# Form I-9, Employment Eligibility Verification - Section 1

I am aware that federal law provides for imprisonment and/or fines for false statements, or the use
of false documents, in connection with the completion of this form. I attest, under penalty of
perjury, that the information I have given above is true and correct, and that I am one of the
following: a citizen of the United States; a noncitizen national of the United States; a lawful
permanent resident; or a noncitizen authorized to work in the United States.

I understand that the employer must examine the original documents I present to establish my
identity and work authorization, and that the employer will complete Section 2 of the official
Form I-9 from those documents.
