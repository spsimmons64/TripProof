# Drug and alcohol policy acknowledgement

You confirm that you have received and read the company's drug and alcohol policy, and that you
understand:

- the company prohibits the use, possession or being under the influence of alcohol or unlawful
  drugs while on duty, on call or in a company vehicle;
- you must tell the company about any prescription or over-the-counter medication that could affect
  your ability to drive safely;
- you may be asked to test before employment, after an accident, at random, on reasonable suspicion,
  and before returning to duty or following up after a violation;
- refusing a test, tampering with a sample or a verified positive result carries the same
  consequences as a positive test; and
- a violation of a State drug law must be reported to the company, which has a written process for
  addressing it.

Drivers who hold a commercial driver's license are additionally covered by 49 CFR part 382, and
this acknowledgement serves as the certificate of receipt required by 49 CFR 382.601(d).
