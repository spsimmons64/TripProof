# Workplace policy acknowledgement

You confirm that you have read and will follow the company's workplace rules, which include:

- **Passengers first.** Treat every passenger with courtesy, assist as needed, and never leave a
  passenger unattended where their safety depends on you.
- **Safety.** Obey traffic law, complete your vehicle checks, use securement equipment as trained,
  and report any accident, incident or vehicle defect immediately.
- **Fitness for duty.** Do not drive when illness, injury, medication, fatigue or anything else
  makes it unsafe.
- **Licensing.** Hold a valid license at all times and tell the office at once if it is suspended,
  cancelled, revoked or restricted.
- **Privacy.** Passenger names, addresses, destinations and health information are confidential.
  Do not discuss, photograph or share them outside your work.
- **Distracted driving.** No hand-held phone use and no texting while driving.
- **Conduct.** No harassment or discrimination, and no weapons, alcohol or unlawful drugs on duty
  or in a company vehicle.

You understand that breaking these rules may lead to discipline, up to and including dismissal.
