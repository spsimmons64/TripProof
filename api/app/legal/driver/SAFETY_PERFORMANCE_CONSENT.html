Consent To Release Safety Performance History

Prospective Employer: {company}

What I Am Consenting To

I, {fullname}, authorize the prospective employer named above, and its authorized agents, to contact my previous employers and to obtain my safety performance history, and I authorize those previous employers to release it.

I understand that the prospective employer is required to investigate, at a minimum, the information listed in 49 CFR 391.23(d) from every employer that employed me to operate a commercial motor vehicle in the previous three years, including general driver identification and employment verification, and the accidents I was involved in that are recorded under 49 CFR 390.15(b)(2) or 390.5.

I further understand that the prospective employer is required to investigate the information listed in 49 CFR 391.23(e) from every employer regulated by the U.S. Department of Transportation that employed me within the previous three years in a safety-sensitive function requiring alcohol and controlled substances testing.

Drug And Alcohol Testing Records

I give my written consent, as required by 49 CFR 40.25(a), for the prospective employer to request my U.S. Department of Transportation drug and alcohol testing records from the employers I worked for in a safety-sensitive function during the previous three years, including:

  • alcohol tests with a result of 0.04 or higher;
  • verified positive drug tests;
  • refusals to be tested, including verified adulterated or substituted results;
  • other violations of the U.S. Department of Transportation drug and alcohol testing regulations; and
  • where a violation occurred, documentation of any successful completion of the return-to-duty process, and the results of any follow-up tests.

I understand that if I refuse to give this consent, the prospective employer must not permit me to perform safety-sensitive functions, including operating a commercial motor vehicle.

My Rights

  • I have the right to review the information provided by my previous employers.
  • I have the right to have errors in that information corrected by the previous employer, and for the corrected information to be re-sent to the prospective employer.
  • I have the right to have my rebuttal attached to my safety performance history where the previous employer and I cannot agree.
  • I understand that the information released is to be kept confidential and used only for the purposes the regulations allow.
