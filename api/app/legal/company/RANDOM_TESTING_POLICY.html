1. PURPOSE

The purpose of this program is to establish the procedures by which the Company administers federally required random controlled substances and alcohol testing for drivers subject to FMCSA regulations.

The program is intended to ensure that covered drivers are properly enrolled, selected through a scientifically valid random process, tested in accordance with federal requirements, and appropriately monitored for ongoing compliance.

2. APPLICABILITY

This program applies to drivers performing safety-sensitive functions involving commercial motor vehicles subject to the CDL requirements and FMCSA drug and alcohol testing regulations under 49 CFR Part 382.

Covered drivers include full-time, part-time, intermittent, temporary, and other drivers performing covered functions for the Company.

Drivers operating vehicles that do not fall within Part 382 coverage shall not be included in the DOT random testing pool solely because they perform NEMT transportation services.

Any non-DOT drug and alcohol testing shall be administered separately.

5. RANDOM TESTING POOL MEMBERSHIP

All drivers subject to the Company's DOT random testing requirements shall be included in an appropriate testing pool.

The Company shall ensure that:

  • Covered drivers are added when they become subject to the program.
  • Drivers no longer performing covered functions are removed when appropriate.
  • Pool records remain accurate and current.
  • All eligible drivers have an equal opportunity for selection.
  • Part 382-covered drivers are not improperly mixed with employees subject only to non-DOT testing.
  • Drivers are not excluded because they work part-time or perform covered functions infrequently.

The Company shall promptly communicate changes in covered-driver status to its testing administrator.

6. MINIMUM ANNUAL TESTING RATES

The Company shall comply with the annual random testing rates established by FMCSA.

Controlled Substances Testing: 50% of the average number of covered driver positions.

Alcohol Testing: 10% of the average number of covered driver positions.

The Company shall monitor applicable DOT announcements and update these rates when required.

Testing percentages represent minimum annual testing activity, not a requirement that a fixed percentage of individual drivers must each be tested exactly once.

A driver may be selected more than once during a calendar year.

7. RANDOM SELECTION PROCEDURES

Random selections shall be performed using a scientifically valid method, such as an appropriately configured computer-based random number generator.

Each covered driver shall have an equal chance of selection each time a selection is conducted.

Selections shall be unannounced and conducted throughout the calendar year.

The Company shall not permit supervisors or administrators to manipulate selections to favor or target particular employees.

Selected drivers shall be tested during the applicable selection period.

8. TESTING SCHEDULE

Random testing shall be reasonably distributed throughout the calendar year.

Testing dates and times shall vary as appropriate to maintain the unannounced nature of the program.

The Company shall not concentrate all random testing into a single period merely to satisfy annual minimum percentages.

9. DRIVER NOTIFICATION

A driver selected for random testing shall be notified through an authorized Company representative.

Upon notification, the driver must proceed immediately to the designated testing location, subject to the applicable regulatory procedures for a driver currently performing a safety-sensitive function.

Drivers shall not receive advance notice of scheduled random selections.

Failure to comply with a valid testing direction may constitute a refusal under applicable DOT regulations.

10. CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES TESTING

Random controlled substances testing shall be conducted in accordance with 49 CFR Part 40.

Specimen collection, laboratory analysis, Medical Review Officer verification, and reporting shall follow applicable DOT requirements.

Only authorized and verified testing results shall be used for regulatory determinations.

11. ALCOHOL TESTING

Random alcohol testing shall be conducted by qualified personnel using approved procedures and equipment.

Random alcohol testing may occur only while the driver is performing safety-sensitive functions, immediately before performing them, or immediately after ceasing such functions.

The Company shall ensure testing is coordinated within the permissible testing window.

12. TESTING COMPLETION AND UNAVAILABLE DRIVERS

The Company shall make reasonable arrangements to ensure selected drivers complete testing during the applicable selection period.

Where a selected driver is unavailable, the Company shall document the circumstances and coordinate with its testing administrator regarding appropriate regulatory procedures.

A selected driver shall not be arbitrarily replaced or excused merely for scheduling convenience.

The Company shall retain documentation of missed, canceled, or incomplete testing events and the actions taken.

13. POSITIVE TESTS, REFUSALS, AND VIOLATIONS

Upon receiving information establishing a DOT drug or alcohol testing violation, the Company shall immediately remove the affected driver from safety-sensitive functions as required by applicable regulations.

The Company shall follow all applicable FMCSA Clearinghouse reporting, Substance Abuse Professional, return-to-duty, and follow-up testing requirements.

The Company shall not permit a driver to resume prohibited safety-sensitive functions until all applicable regulatory conditions have been satisfied.

14. RECORDKEEPING

The Company shall maintain records required by 49 CFR §382.401, including, as applicable:

  • Covered-driver pool membership records.
  • Random selection documentation.
  • Testing dates and completion records.
  • Verified testing results.
  • Documentation of refusals and violations.
  • Testing administration records.
  • C/TPA records and reports.
  • Annual testing summaries.
  • Required corrective-action documentation.

Records shall be maintained securely with controlled access.

Applicable federal retention periods shall be followed, including the different periods established for testing results, collection records, program administration, and training records.

15. CONFIDENTIALITY

DOT drug and alcohol testing records shall be treated as confidential.

Access shall be limited to authorized personnel and permitted service agents.

Records shall be disclosed only as authorized or required by applicable DOT regulations.

The Company shall implement appropriate safeguards for electronic and physical records.

16. ANNUAL PROGRAM REVIEW

The Company shall periodically review its random testing program to confirm:

  • Covered drivers are properly enrolled.
  • Random selections use a compliant method.
  • Testing is distributed throughout the year.
  • Required annual testing rates are being met.
  • Testing records are complete and accessible.
  • C/TPA arrangements remain active, where applicable.
  • Identified deficiencies are corrected.

The Company shall document corrective actions when program deficiencies are identified.

17. SERVICE AGENT OVERSIGHT

Where a C/TPA administers the random testing program, the Company shall ensure that the administrator performs its contracted functions in accordance with applicable DOT requirements.

The Company shall obtain sufficient documentation to demonstrate its compliance during authorized audits and inspections.

Use of a C/TPA does not relieve the Company of its regulatory responsibilities.

18. PROGRAM CHANGES

This program shall be reviewed and revised when applicable federal regulations change, the Company's testing arrangements materially change, or operational circumstances require modification.

The Company shall maintain documentation of material revisions and their effective dates.
