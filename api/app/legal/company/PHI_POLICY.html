DRIVER PHI CONFIDENTIALITY AND PRIVACY POLICY

Company: {company}

Effective Date: {effectivedate}

1. PURPOSE

{company} transports passengers who may be receiving health care, and in the course of that work its drivers and staff come into contact with confidential information about those passengers.

This policy sets out how that information is to be handled. It applies to Protected Health Information ("PHI") as defined by the HIPAA Privacy Rule where the Company is a covered entity or business associate, and to other confidential personal information whether or not HIPAA reaches it.

Each driver signs an acknowledgment of this policy. That acknowledgment is kept on the driver's own file; this document is what they acknowledged.

2. WHO THIS POLICY COVERS

This policy applies to every driver, dispatcher, supervisor, administrative employee, maintenance employee, temporary employee, contractor, and other person who may access passenger information on behalf of {company}.

3. WHAT IS PROTECTED

Protected and confidential information includes:

  • Passenger names, addresses, telephone numbers, dates of birth, and other identifying information;
  • Medicaid, Medicare, insurance, member, or identification numbers;
  • Medical appointments, treatment locations, medical facilities, providers, or destinations;
  • Information concerning a passenger's medical condition, disability, mobility needs, treatment, or health status;
  • Trip information that reveals or could reveal that an individual is receiving health care or medical services;
  • Information held in dispatch systems, scheduling systems, mobile applications, manifests, trip sheets, electronic communications, or other Company records; and
  • Conversations or communications concerning passengers and their health care or transportation.

The fact that a particular person was transported to a particular place can itself disclose health information, and is protected on the same terms as a medical record.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

Everyone covered by this policy shall:

  • Access confidential information only when necessary to perform assigned duties;
  • Use or disclose information only for authorized business purposes and only to persons authorized to receive it;
  • Protect passenger information from unauthorized access, viewing, disclosure, loss, or theft;
  • Not discuss passengers, their medical conditions, appointments, destinations, or other confidential information with friends, family members, other passengers, unauthorized employees, or other unauthorized persons;
  • Avoid discussing confidential passenger information where conversations may reasonably be overheard;
  • Protect trip manifests, schedules, passenger lists, mobile devices, paperwork, and other materials containing confidential information;
  • Not photograph, screenshot, copy, download, transmit, post, or retain passenger information except when specifically authorized for a legitimate business purpose;
  • Not post passenger information, photographs, trip details, or other confidential information on social media or other public platforms;
  • Use only authorized systems, applications, devices, and communication methods;
  • Protect passwords, PINs, and other credentials, and not permit another person to use them;
  • Secure or properly dispose of documents and materials containing confidential information; and
  • Immediately report to a supervisor or to {company} management any suspected or actual loss, theft, unauthorized access or disclosure, misdirected communication, compromised device, or other privacy or security incident.

5. MINIMUM NECESSARY

Only the minimum amount of confidential information reasonably necessary to perform the transportation service or other authorized function may be accessed, used, or communicated.

Transporting a passenger does not permit anybody to access, inquire about, discuss, or disclose that passenger's medical information beyond what is reasonably necessary to perform the assigned duty safely and properly.

6. REPORTING AN INCIDENT

A suspected or actual privacy or security incident must be reported to a supervisor or to {company} management immediately. Reporting is required whether or not the person reporting believes the information was actually seen or misused, and whether or not the incident was anybody's fault.

{company} will investigate, will take the steps the circumstances and applicable law require, and will not retaliate against anybody for reporting an incident in good faith.

7. CONTINUING OBLIGATION

The obligation to protect confidential information continues after a trip has ended and after employment, contract, or other relationship with {company} ends.

Passenger information shall not be retained, accessed, used, or disclosed once it is no longer required for an authorized duty.

8. TRAINING

{company} shall train the people covered by this policy on their responsibilities under it, and shall keep a record of that training.

9. VIOLATIONS

Unauthorized access, use, disclosure, copying, retention, or distribution of confidential information may violate this policy, contractual requirements, and applicable law.

Violations may result in corrective or disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or of a contractual relationship, as well as other consequences permitted by applicable law.

10. POLICY ADMINISTRATION

Questions concerning this policy should be directed to {company} management.

{company} reserves the right to amend this policy to maintain compliance with changes in federal or state law.
