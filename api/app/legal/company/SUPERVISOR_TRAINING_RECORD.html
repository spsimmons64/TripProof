3. TRAINING REQUIREMENTS

The supervisor identified above must complete training designed to recognise the circumstances that may establish reasonable suspicion of alcohol misuse or controlled substances use by a driver subject to 49 CFR Part 382.

The training must include the following:

Alcohol misuse recognition - minimum 60 minutes. Instruction covering the physical, behavioural, speech and performance indicators of probable alcohol misuse.

Controlled substances use recognition - minimum 60 minutes. Instruction covering the physical, behavioural, speech and performance indicators of probable controlled substances use.

Total required training: 120 minutes.
