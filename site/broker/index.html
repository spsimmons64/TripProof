The exposure you carry

When an auditor asks, the answer should already be on file.

When an auditor asks who drove a trip, whether they were qualified that day, and whether the trip happened at all, the answer usually lives in a provider's paperwork. TripProof makes that answer part of every trip.

Unqualified drivers

An expired license, a lapsed screening or missing training is easy to miss on a spreadsheet. In TripProof it stops the assignment, with the reason stated.

Trips you cannot verify

Pickup and drop-off times come from the driver's phone at the moment they happen, with a GPS trace of the route, not from a form filled in at the end of the day.

Records that change quietly

Every assignment, edit and cancellation is kept with who made it, when, and why. History is added to, never overwritten.

How a trip is proven

Checked before it is assigned. Recorded while it runs. Packaged when it is done.

Before the trip

Only qualified drivers and vehicles can be assigned

  • Licenses, driving records, exclusion screening, drug and alcohol status and training, checked against the rules in force that day.
  • Vehicles checked for registration, insurance, inspection and open defects, and matched to the service level.
  • Your contract can add requirements of its own, applied to your trips only.
  • A failed check refuses the assignment. It is not a warning that can be clicked past.
Federal rules (FMCSA, OIG) and state NEMT programs, starting with Florida and Georgia
During the trip

The driver's app records what happens, as it happens

  • On the way, arrived, passenger aboard and arrived at destination, each stamped with time and position.
  • A pre-trip vehicle check is required before setting off where the rules call for one.
  • Waiting time and problems on the road are reported to the office in the moment.
  • Will-call returns stay unassigned until the passenger actually calls.
Times are the device's own, never later than the server's clock
After the trip

One record that answers an audit

  • The route driven on a map, from GPS positions reported during the leg.
  • The full timeline: assignments, changes, events, waits and problems, each with its source.
  • Which requirements were checked, under which rule version, and what evidence was on file.
  • Audit packets can be exported for a date range or a set of trips.
Trips with a failed requirement can be held from billing

Security and privacy

Member information is handled as protected health information.

Encrypted at restMember identity, addresses from broker files and Social Security numbers are stored encrypted.
Every read of member details is loggedViewing protected details writes an entry to the access log, under the person who viewed them.
An activity log that cannot be editedThe log is append-only: entries can be added, never changed or deleted.
Each provider's data is its ownAccounts are separated, and every request is checked against the account it belongs to.
Signatures tied to identityElectronic signatures are adopted with date of birth and Social Security number, and one identity cannot sign as another.
Sessions that endOffice sessions close when the browser tab closes and time out after an hour idle.
