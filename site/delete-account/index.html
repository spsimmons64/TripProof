Delete Your TripProofDriver Login

TripProofDriver is published by Arrowleaf Technologies, LLC, operator of TripProof Online.

In The App

  • Open TripProofDriver and sign in.
  • Tap the account button at the top right.
  • Tap Delete My Login, then Yes.

Your login is deleted at once and the app signs out.

Without The App

Email support@tripproofonline.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my TripProofDriver login" and the name of the company you drive for. We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What Is Deleted

  • Your password and your ability to sign in.
  • Every phone registered to receive your trip notifications.

What Is Kept

The company you drive for keeps its records of the trips you drove (times, locations recorded during those trips, passenger signatures and reported problems), your vehicle checks, and your driver qualification file. Transportation companies are required to keep these by law and by their broker and Medicaid contracts, for as long as those require. Ask your company about them.

Your company can invite you back to the app later; that creates a new login.
