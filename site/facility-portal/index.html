A Scheduling Portal for Your Facility Partners

Give each authorized healthcare facility its own access to TripProof Online.

Facility users can:

  • Log into their facility's transportation portal.
  • Access information associated with their authorized facility.
  • Submit transportation requests with pickup and drop-off details.
  • Enter transportation information directly into the system.

Facility users do not receive unrestricted access to your transportation company's operations or other facilities' information.

Facilities Request. Dispatch Decides.

A transportation request is not a confirmed trip.

Every facility-submitted transportation request enters the same dispatch acceptance workflow used for broker-submitted trips.

Your dispatch team reviews each request and decides whether to accept or reject it.

This ensures that healthcare facilities can conveniently request transportation without automatically committing your company to trips it hasn't approved.

How It Works

How Facility Scheduling Works

1

Facility Login

An authorized facility employee signs into TripProof Online using their facility account.

2

Transportation Request

The facility submits a trip request with the necessary pickup and drop-off information.

3

Dispatch Review

The request appears in the transportation provider's dispatch workflow for acceptance or rejection.

4

Trip Assignment

Accepted trips enter the transportation company's scheduling and assignment process, subject to the applicable driver, vehicle, and trip requirements.

5

Connected Trip Records

Facility transportation requests become part of the same operational system used to manage other NEMT transportation activities.

Benefits

Less Administrative Work. Better Facility Relationships.

The Facility Scheduling Portal is designed to help NEMT companies:

  • Reduce transportation requests received through disconnected communication channels.
  • Minimize duplicate data entry by dispatch personnel.
  • Offer healthcare facilities a convenient way to request rides.
  • Maintain control over trip acceptance and scheduling.
  • Manage facility transportation alongside broker and private-pay trips.
  • Strengthen relationships with healthcare organizations that regularly need transportation services.
