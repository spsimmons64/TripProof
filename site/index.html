Part 1

Four jobs. One record.

Most transport companies run dispatch in one place, driver files in another and billing in a third. TripProof does all four, so the trip and the proof of it are the same record.

§ 01

Dispatch

Book, assign and run the day from one board.

  • Auto Assign the day's trips to the drivers you have
  • Will-calls, on-call cover, facility closing times
  • A driver out sick, off their trips in one action
§ 02

Compliance

Every driver and vehicle file, checked the day of the trip.

  • Licenses, driving records, screenings, training
  • Federal exclusion lists screened every day
  • Federal, state NEMT and broker contract rules
§ 03

Driver App

The driver's phone records the trip as it happens.

§ 04

Billing

From a driven trip to money in the bank.

  • Priced from your fee schedules
  • Broker batches, invoices, online payment
  • Short pays and denials tracked until settled
Part 2

A stop is a stop.

When a driver stops being qualified, TripProof does not flash a warning someone can click past. The assignment is refused, the reason is named, and dispatch is told.

Caught the moment it changes

A record read, a screening returned, a license lapsed: every trip that driver holds in the next month is checked again on the spot.

Flagged where dispatch looks

The trips go to the top of the board with the reason, and whoever takes dispatch alerts gets a text.

Part 3

Checked before. Recorded during. Sealed after.

A. Before The Trip

Only a qualified driver, in a vehicle that fits, can be assigned

  • Driver and vehicle checked against federal rules, state NEMT programs and the broker's contract.
  • Wheelchair trips only go to lift vehicles and trained drivers.
  • Pickups outside your hours need someone on call first.
B. During The Trip

The driver's phone writes the record as it happens

  • On the way, arrived, aboard and dropped off, each with time and position.
  • Waiting time and problems on the road reach the office in the moment.
  • The passenger signs at the drop-off, or the driver says why not.
C. After The Trip

One record that answers an audit, and gets you paid

  • The route on a map, the full timeline and the rules each check passed under.
  • Priced, billed and tracked to payment, with the proof behind every line.
  • History is added to, never quietly changed.
