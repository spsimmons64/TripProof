Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026. Covers tripproofonline.com, the TripProof Online web application and the TripProofDriver app for Android and iPhone.

Who We Are

TripProof Online is operated by Arrowleaf Technologies, LLC ("we"). It provides dispatch, compliance and billing software to non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) companies. A transportation company (your "company") opens an account with us and adds its office staff and drivers. For the information your company keeps about its drivers, passengers and trips, your company decides what is collected and why, and we process it on its behalf.

Questions about this policy: support@tripproofonline.com or (727) 207-5512.

What The TripProofDriver App Collects

  • Your login: your name, email address and mobile number as your company recorded them, and a password you set. The password is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Precise location, while you are signed in: the phone's position, including when the app is in the background or the screen is off, from the moment you sign in until you sign out. On Android a notification stays on screen the whole time to say so. Location is not collected while you are signed out.
  • What happens on a trip: the times and places you mark a trip on the way, arrived, passenger on board and completed; waiting time and its reason; problems you report; and the notes you add.
  • Passenger signatures: a signature drawn on the phone at drop-off, or the reason none was taken.
  • Vehicle checks: the pre-trip inspection results you file.
  • Your phone, for notifications: a notification token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and whether the phone is Android or iPhone.
  • Network details: the IP address a sign-in or a login deletion came from.

The app shows you passenger names, addresses and phone numbers for the trips you are given. That information belongs to your company and its brokers and is treated as protected health information.

What The Website And Web Application Collect

When someone signs up to be a tester on tripproofonline.com we keep what they enter on the form and the IP address it came from. Office users of the web application provide their name, email address and, if they choose, a mobile number for alerts. The web application keeps an activity log of who did what, and a log of who viewed protected details.

How It Is Used

  • To let your company's office see where its vehicles are, give you trips and tell you when a trip changes.
  • To record that each trip happened, where and when, and to give your company proof of service for its brokers, Medicaid programs and auditors.
  • To keep your company's driver and vehicle compliance records.
  • To send you notifications and text messages about your trips.

We do not sell information, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites. The app has no advertising or analytics code.

Who It Is Shared With

  • Your company, which can see everything described above about its own drivers and trips.
  • Brokers and payers your company does trips for, which receive the trip records they are owed as proof of service.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers notifications to the app. It receives the notification token and the message, which names the trip and its time and nothing about the passenger.
  • Bandwidth or Twilio, which deliver text messages. They receive your mobile number and the message, which likewise names only the trip and its time.
  • Our email provider, for invitations and password links.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How Long It Is Kept

Trip records, locations recorded during trips, signatures and compliance records are kept for as long as your company is required to keep them by law and by its broker and Medicaid contracts. Notification tokens stop being used when you sign out or delete your login. Tester sign-ups are kept until the tester program ends or the person asks us to remove them.

Deleting Your Login

In the TripProofDriver app, tap the account button at the top right, then Delete My Login, then Yes. Your login, password and notification registrations are deleted at once and the app signs out. You can also ask by email; see Delete Your TripProofDriver Login.

Deleting your login does not delete your company's records of the trips you drove or of your qualifications to drive, which your company is required to keep. Ask your company about those records.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Social Security numbers and protected member details are stored encrypted. Each company's information is kept separate, and every request is checked against the account it belongs to.

Children

TripProof Online and the TripProofDriver app are for transportation companies and their employees and are not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

When this policy changes we will update the effective date at the top of this page, and tell companies on their account before a change that affects how drivers' information is used.
